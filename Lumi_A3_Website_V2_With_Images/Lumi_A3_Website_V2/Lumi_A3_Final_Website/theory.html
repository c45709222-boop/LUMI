FROM CONSUMPTION TO PARTICIPATION

The audience makes a connection.

The website's interactive structure changes the audience's role from following a finished narrative to assembling one interpretation from distributed clues. This is where the website contributes something that the other platforms cannot do in the same way.

NON-LINEARITY

Choose the path

Evidence can be opened in different orders, so discovery becomes part of the experience.

INTERACTIVITY

The audience can submit a secret or theory instead of only reading Rachel's conclusions.
