TEAM INTEGRATION POINT
When teammates finish their final media, insert the actual original assets or working links into the relevant cards. Keep the narrative roles distinct: Instagram = Rachel's present; video = Lumi's past; hand-drawn material = old legend; website = investigation + audience participation.
MIGRATORY CUES

Clues should travel.

A date, secret number, phrase, visual mark or repeated object can point an audience from one platform to another. The website should never simply say “go watch the video”; it should give the audience a reason to move.

CUE 01

Secret numbers

Secret #001 and Secret #002 can recur across media and identify the same narrative event from different perspectives.

CUE 02

Dates

“Six years ago” and “eight months ago” can appear as shared anchors that allow audiences to match records across platforms.

CUE 03

Repeated phrases

A sentence or phrase can become a clue that only makes sense after the audience discovers it elsewhere.
